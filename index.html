From our box to your table in four steps

Order by Thursday and your box arrives on the delivery day you pick.

  1. 1

    Pick your recipes

    Browse the weekly menu in the Zad app or on the website and choose what you feel like cooking.

  2. 2

    Make it yours

    Set servings, swap the protein and choose how much spice. The ingredients adjust to match.

  3. 3

    We pack and deliver

    Everything is weighed, packed in a chilled box and brought to your door, with a printed recipe card.

  4. 4

    Cook and enjoy

    Follow the card or scan the QR code for a short video. Most dishes are ready in under an hour.

      Questions, answered

      Where do you deliver?

      We're starting in Doha, Al Wakrah and Al Rayyan, with more of Qatar to follow. Enter your area at checkout to see your delivery days.

      How fresh is everything when it arrives?

      Produce is packed the day before delivery and travels in an insulated, chilled box. Meat and seafood stay cold for up to 8 hours after drop-off.

      Can I skip a week or cancel?

      Yes. Skip, pause or cancel from the app any time before Thursday at midnight. There are no fees or minimum commitments.

      What if I have allergies?

      Each recipe lists all 14 major allergens. You can filter the menu by allergen, and swap ingredients on many dishes.

      What happens to the packaging?

      Boxes are recyclable cardboard and our ice packs are reusable. Hand them back to the driver on your next delivery and we'll reuse them.
